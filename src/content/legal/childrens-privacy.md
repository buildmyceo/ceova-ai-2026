## 1. Introduction

This Children’s Privacy Policy (“Policy”) explains how CEOVA (“CEOVA”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information relating to children and young users.

This Policy should be read together with our:

* Privacy Policy
* Terms & Conditions
* Data Retention & Deletion Policy

---

## 2. Minimum Age

CEOVA is intended for users who are 13 years of age or older.

If the laws of your country require a higher minimum age or parental consent, you must satisfy those legal requirements before using CEOVA.

By using CEOVA, you represent that you meet the applicable age requirements.

---

## 3. Users Under the Age of Digital Consent

Where applicable law requires parental or legal guardian consent, users below the applicable age of digital consent must obtain such consent before using CEOVA.

Parents or guardians are responsible for supervising the child’s use of the Services.

---

## 4. Information We Collect

For eligible young users, CEOVA may collect:

* Name
* Email address
* Profile picture
* Account information
* Chat history
* Uploaded files
* AI-generated content
* Connected account information
* Subscription information (if applicable)
* Device and usage information

CEOVA collects only the information reasonably necessary to provide the Services.

---

## 5. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

CEOVA does not intentionally collect:

* Information from children below the minimum permitted age without required consent.
* Government identification unless legally required.
* Banking passwords.
* Google passwords.
* One-Time Passwords (OTPs).
* Security question answers.

Users should never share passwords or other sensitive credentials through CEOVA.

---

## 6. AI Services

Children and young users should understand that AI-generated responses:

* May be inaccurate.
* May be incomplete.
* May be outdated.
* Should not replace advice from parents, teachers, or qualified professionals.

Important decisions should always be reviewed by a responsible adult where appropriate.

CEOVA may be used for educational and learning purposes.

* Homework assistance.
* Studying.
* Research.
* Document understanding.

Users remain responsible for verifying AI-generated information before relying upon it.

## 8. Parental Rights

Where applicable law grants parents or legal guardians specific rights, they may request:

* Access to a child’s personal information.
* Correction of inaccurate information.
* Deletion of personal information.
* Termination of the child’s account.
* Withdrawal of consent where applicable.

Reasonable verification of identity may be required before processing such requests.

---

## 9. Account Deletion

Parents, guardians, or eligible users may request permanent deletion of an account.

Upon deletion, CEOVA will make commercially reasonable efforts to remove:

* Account information.
* Chat history.
* Uploaded files.
* AI-generated files.
* Connected accounts.

Certain information may be retained where required by applicable law.

## 10. Safety

CEOVA is committed to providing a safe environment for young users.

We prohibit the use of CEOVA for:

* Child exploitation.
* Child sexual abuse material.
* Grooming.
* Harassment.
* Bullying.
* Threats.
* Illegal activities targeting children.

Accounts violating these rules may be immediately suspended or permanently terminated.

Where required by law, CEOVA may report illegal content or activity to the appropriate authorities.
