# CEOVA Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Effective Date:** 26/july/2026  
**Last Updated:** 26/july/2026

---

## 1. Introduction

This Refund & Cancellation Policy (“Policy”) explains how subscription cancellations, refunds, payment disputes, billing issues, and related matters are handled for CEOVA (“CEOVA”, “we”, “our”, or “us”).

By purchasing a CEOVA subscription, you agree to this Policy in addition to our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Subscription Plans

CEOVA currently offers:

* Free Plan
* Monthly Subscription
* Yearly Subscription

Additional plans, storage add-ons, enterprise subscriptions, or other services may be introduced in the future.

---

## 3. Automatic Renewal

All paid subscriptions automatically renew at the end of the selected billing period unless cancelled before the renewal date.

By purchasing a subscription, you authorize recurring billing until cancellation.

---

## 4. Cancelling Your Subscription

You may cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings or by contacting CEOVA Support.

Cancellation will:

* Stop future automatic renewals.
* Allow continued access to paid features until the end of the current billing cycle.
* Not automatically generate a refund.

Deleting your CEOVA account does not automatically cancel recurring subscriptions. Users are responsible for cancelling their subscription before deleting their account.

---

## 5. Refund Eligibility

CEOVA generally does not provide refunds once a subscription has started.

However, refund requests may be considered if all of the following conditions are met:

* The request is submitted within 2 hours of the original purchase.
* The subscription was purchased directly through an authorized CEOVA payment method.
* Payment can be successfully verified.
* The request complies with this Policy.

Submitting a request does not guarantee approval.

---

## 6. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, users must send an email to both of the following addresses:

**Primary Email**

contact@ceova.online

**Support Email**

ceova.ai@gmail.com

The email must include:

* Registered CEOVA email address.
* Payment ID or Transaction ID.
* Date and time of purchase.
* Subscription plan purchased.
* Reason for requesting the refund.
* Any additional information reasonably requested for verification.

Incomplete requests may delay or prevent processing.

---

## 7. Refund Processing Fee

If a refund request is approved, CEOVA may deduct a processing fee of up to 1% of the original payment amount to cover non-refundable payment processing charges imposed by payment providers.

The exact deduction, if any, may vary depending on the payment method, payment provider, or applicable processing costs.

---

## 8. Refund Approval

Refund approval is entirely at CEOVA’s discretion except where applicable law requires a refund.

Refund requests may be approved for reasons including:

* Duplicate payments.
* Accidental purchases.
* Incorrect billing.
* Technical billing errors caused by CEOVA.
* Other circumstances determined appropriate by CEOVA.

---

## 9. Refund Rejection

Refund requests may be rejected if:

* Submitted more than 2 hours after purchase.
* Payment cannot be verified.
* Fraud or abuse is suspected.
* False or misleading information is provided.
* The purchase violates CEOVA’s Terms & Conditions.
* The payment provider rejects or reverses the transaction.
* Applicable law does not require a refund and the request does not satisfy this Policy.

---

## 10. Chargebacks

Before initiating a chargeback with your payment provider or financial institution, users are encouraged to contact CEOVA Support.

Unnecessary or fraudulent chargebacks may result in:

* Temporary account suspension.
* Permanent account termination.
* Recovery of unpaid amounts where permitted by law.

---

## 11. Payment Disputes

Users experiencing payment issues should contact CEOVA before contacting their bank whenever reasonably possible.

CEOVA will make reasonable efforts to resolve billing issues promptly.

---

## 12. Failed Payments

If a subscription renewal payment fails:

* CEOVA may retry the payment.
* Access to premium features may be suspended until payment is successfully completed.
* Continued failure may result in automatic downgrade to the Free Plan.
