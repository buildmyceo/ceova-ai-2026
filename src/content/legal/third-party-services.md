## 3. User Authorization

Before CEOVA can access a third-party service, you must explicitly authorize the connection.

Authorization is generally performed using secure authentication methods such as OAuth.

By authorizing an integration, you grant CEOVA permission to perform only the actions necessary to provide the requested functionality.

---

## 4. Permissions

CEOVA requests only the permissions reasonably necessary for each integration.

Examples include:

* Reading Gmail messages.
* Sending Gmail messages.
* Accessing Google Drive files.
* Creating Calendar events.
* Sending Telegram messages.

CEOVA does not intentionally request unnecessary permissions.

---

## 5. OAuth Authentication

Most supported integrations use OAuth.

CEOVA:

* Does not collect your third-party passwords.
* Does not store your Google password.
* Does not ask for banking passwords.
* Stores only the access tokens and refresh tokens required to provide the requested functionality.

Tokens are stored using appropriate security measures.

Users remain in control of connected accounts.

You may:

* Connect an account.
* Disconnect an account.
* Revoke authorization.
* Remove integrations.
* Stop synchronization.

Revoking authorization may prevent CEOVA from providing related functionality.

## 7. Data Processing
