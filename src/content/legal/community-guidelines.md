# CEOVA Community Guidelines & Content Policy

**Effective Date:** 26/july/2026  
**Last Updated:** 26/july/2026

---

## 1. Introduction

These Community Guidelines & Content Policy (“Guidelines”) explain the standards of behavior expected from every user of CEOVA (“CEOVA”, “we”, “our”, or “us”).

Our goal is to provide a safe, respectful, productive, and trustworthy environment where individuals and businesses can use AI responsibly.

By using CEOVA, you agree to follow these Guidelines in addition to our:

* Terms & Conditions
* Privacy Policy
* Acceptable Use Policy
* AI Usage Policy

Failure to follow these Guidelines may result in content removal, account restrictions, suspension, termination, or legal action where appropriate.

---

## 2. Our Community Principles

CEOVA encourages users to:

* Use AI responsibly.
* Respect other users.
* Respect intellectual property.
* Follow applicable laws.
* Use CEOVA for productive and lawful purposes.
* Report abuse and security concerns.
* Help improve the platform through constructive feedback.

---

## 3. Respectful Conduct

Users must interact respectfully with CEOVA and others.

You must not:

* Harass others.
* Threaten others.
* Intimidate others.
* Bully others.
* Promote violence.
* Encourage self-harm.
* Encourage criminal activity.
* Abuse customer support staff.
* Repeatedly disrupt platform operations.

---

## 4. Content Standards

Users are responsible for all content uploaded, generated, stored, or shared through CEOVA.

Content should be:

* Lawful.
* Respectful.
* Accurate where appropriate.
* Free from malicious intent.
* Respectful of intellectual property rights.

---

## 5. Prohibited Content

The following content is prohibited:

**Illegal Content**

Including but not limited to:

* Child sexual abuse material.
* Human trafficking.
* Terrorist propaganda.
* Illegal drug trafficking.
* Illegal weapons trafficking.
* Fraud.
* Counterfeit documents.
* Identity theft.
* Phishing.
* Financial scams.
* Malware.
* Ransomware.
* Stolen credentials.

**Harmful Content**

Including:

* Violent threats.
* Harassment.
* Hate speech prohibited by applicable law.
* Doxxing.
* Blackmail.
* Extortion.
* Encouragement of illegal activity.

**Malicious Content**

Including:

* Malware.
* Viruses.
* Spyware.
* Worms.
* Exploit code.
* Harmful scripts.
* Files intended to compromise security.

**Intellectual Property Violations**

Including:

* Copyright infringement.
* Trademark infringement.
* Unauthorized distribution.
* Pirated software.
* Stolen digital assets.

---

## 6. AI Usage Standards

Users should use CEOVA AI responsibly.

Users should not:

* Generate illegal material.
* Generate malicious code for unlawful purposes.
* Use AI to deceive or defraud.
* Attempt to bypass AI safety systems.
* Abuse AI rate limits.
* Misrepresent AI-generated content as verified fact without review.

AI responses should always be reviewed before use.

---

## 7. File Upload Standards

Only upload files:

* You own.
* You are authorized to use.
* That comply with applicable law.
* That do not threaten platform security.

CEOVA may reject unsupported or unsafe files.

---

## 8. Public Sharing

CEOVA keeps user content private by default.

If users create public share links:

* Users are responsible for the information they choose to share.
* Public links may be viewed by anyone with access to the link.
* Users may revoke public links at any time.

CEOVA is not responsible for information intentionally shared publicly by users.

---

## 9. Business Use

Businesses using CEOVA should:

* Respect customer privacy.
* Obtain required permissions.
* Use AI responsibly.
* Verify AI-generated outputs before relying on them.
* Comply with applicable regulations.

---

Users are encouraged to report:

* Illegal content.
* Abuse.
* Security vulnerabilities.
* Copyright infringement.
* Fraud.
* Harassment.
* Child safety concerns.

## 11. Enforcement

CEOVA may take one or more of the following actions:

* Issue a warning.
* Remove content.
* Restrict features.
* Suspend the account.
* Permanently terminate the account.
* Report illegal activity to competent authorities where required by law.

Enforcement decisions are made based on the severity, frequency, and nature of the violation.

---

## 12. Appeals

If you believe enforcement action was taken in error, you may submit an appeal by contacting:

contact@ceova.online

Appeals should include:

* Your registered email address.
* Details of the action being appealed.
* Any supporting information.

CEOVA will review appeals in good faith but reserves the final decision.
