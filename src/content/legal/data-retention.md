# CEOVA Data Retention & Deletion Policy

**Effective Date:** 26/july/2026  
**Last Updated:** 26/july/2026

---

## 1. Introduction

This Data Retention & Deletion Policy (“Policy”) explains how CEOVA (“CEOVA”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) retains, stores, archives, deletes, and manages user data.

This Policy should be read together with our:

* Privacy Policy
* Terms & Conditions
* Security Policy
* AI Usage Policy

By using CEOVA, you acknowledge that your information may be retained and deleted in accordance with this Policy.

---

## 2. Purpose

This Policy exists to:

* Protect user privacy.
* Ensure transparency.
* Comply with applicable laws.
* Maintain platform security.
* Prevent fraud.
* Support account recovery where applicable.
* Meet legal and financial obligations.

---

## 3. Data We Retain

Depending on your use of CEOVA, we may retain:

* Account information.
* Profile information.
* Chat history.
* Uploaded files.
* AI-generated files.
* Connected account authorizations.
* OAuth tokens.
* Subscription records.
* Billing records.
* Usage logs.
* Security logs.
* Customer support communications.
* Device information.
* Feature usage statistics.

---

## 4. Account Information

We retain:

* Name
* Email address
* Profile picture
* Account ID
* Subscription status
* Connected services
* Account preferences

**Retention Period:**

Until the account is permanently deleted, unless longer retention is required by law.

---

## 5. Chat History

Conversation history is stored to provide continuity and user access.

**Retention Period:**

* Until deleted by the user.
* Or until the user’s account is permanently deleted.

Users remain in control of deleting their own conversations.

---

## 6. Uploaded Files

Files uploaded by users are stored securely in CEOVA Cloud Storage.

Examples include:

* Images
* PDFs
* Documents
* Videos
* Audio
* Archives
* Spreadsheets
* Presentations

**Retention Period:**

Until deleted by the user or upon permanent account deletion.

---

## 7. AI-Generated Files

AI-generated files may include:

* OCR results.
* Converted files.
* Generated PDFs.
* AI summaries.
* Processed images.
* Other outputs created by CEOVA.

**Retention Period:**

Until deleted by the user or upon permanent account deletion.

Users retain ownership of AI-generated files.

---

## 8. Connected Services

Information required to maintain authorized integrations may be retained, including:

* OAuth access tokens.
* OAuth refresh tokens.
* Connector configuration.
* Synchronization settings.

**Retention Period:**

Until:

* The user disconnects the service.
* The user revokes authorization.
* The account is deleted.

Sensitive authentication tokens are stored using appropriate security measures.

---

## 9. Billing Records

Billing records may include:

* Payment ID
* Transaction ID
* Subscription history
* Invoice records
* Refund history

These records may be retained for the period required by applicable tax, accounting, financial, or legal obligations.

---

## 10. Security Logs

CEOVA maintains security logs to:

* Detect fraud.
* Investigate abuse.
* Prevent attacks.
* Protect users.
* Improve security.

Examples include:

* Login attempts.
* Authentication events.
* API requests.
* Error logs.
* Security alerts.

Retention periods may vary depending on operational and legal requirements.

---

## 11. Customer Support Records

Support requests, emails, and communications may be retained to:

* Resolve disputes.
* Improve customer support.
* Investigate technical issues.
* Maintain service quality.

Support records may be deleted when no longer required.

---

## 12. Account Deletion

Users may permanently delete their CEOVA account at any time.

Upon deletion, CEOVA will make commercially reasonable efforts to remove:

* Account profile.
* Chat history.
* Uploaded files.
* AI-generated files.
* Connected services.
* Authentication data.

Deletion may take a reasonable period to complete across active systems and backups.

---

## 13. Information That May Be Retained

Even after account deletion, CEOVA may retain limited information where necessary to:

* Comply with applicable law.
* Meet tax obligations.
* Meet accounting requirements.
* Resolve disputes.
* Prevent fraud.
* Protect platform security.
* Enforce legal rights.
* Respond to lawful requests from authorities.

Only the minimum necessary information will be retained.

---

## 14. Backups

To maintain service reliability, CEOVA may create encrypted backups of certain systems.

Deleted information may temporarily remain within secure backups until backup rotation or expiration.

Backups are not used to restore deleted user accounts except where necessary for disaster recovery or legal obligations.

---

## 15. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

* Downloading any files they wish to keep before deleting their account.
* Managing their cloud storage.
* Deleting unwanted content.
* Disconnecting third-party accounts where desired.

Account deletion is generally irreversible.

---

## 16. Legal Requests

CEOVA may preserve or disclose information when required to:

* Comply with applicable law.
* Respond to court orders.
* Cooperate with lawful investigations.
* Protect CEOVA, its users, or the public.
* Enforce our legal rights.

---

CEOVA may update this Policy from time to time to reflect:

* Legal requirements.
* Operational changes.
* Security improvements.
* Infrastructure updates.
* New services.

Material updates may be communicated through the application, website, or email where appropriate.
